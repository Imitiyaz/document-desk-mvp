# Community conduct

Author: **Imitiyaz**. AI assistance: **AI Agent**. [Attribution](AUTHORS.md).

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique code and evidence without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Do not share confidential documents, credentials or personal details in public discussions. Use private vulnerability reporting for security-sensitive issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when necessary. Report concerns privately using a maintainer's published contact channel; avoid repeating sensitive material in public.
